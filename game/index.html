EMBERFALL
DUEL

A card duel of four Houses against Briar, the Thicket Warden.

How to play

  • Hearts: you and Briar start on ♥ 20. Reduce hers to 0 to win — protect yours.
  • Energy ◆ grows by 1 each of your turns (max 8) and pays for every card.
  • Champions stay on the table and attack from next turn (unless Swift). Guard champions must be attacked first.
  • Kindle 🔥: play a card while a champion of its House is already on your table and it enters stronger — champions +1/+1, Tricks boosted.
  • Click a card to play it, click one of your ready champions, then click its target. END TURN hands over to Briar.

An original game — its cards, houses and art were made for this table.

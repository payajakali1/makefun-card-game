    EMBERFALL
    DUEL

    A card duel of four Houses, fought over a falling ember.

    ▲ Ember ● Tide ✚ Thorn ◆ Gloom

    Both duelists start at ♥ 20 Heart. Champions fight from the table; Tricks strike at once. Play a second card of a House in one turn and it is Kindled 🔥 — stronger for free. First Heart to 0 loses.

    Mouse and keyboard: click to play and attack · 1–9 picks a card · E ends the turn · Esc cancels.

    VICTORY

    How to play

    • Goal: reduce Vesper's Heart from ♥ 20 to 0 before she reduces yours.
    • Energy ◆ grows by 1 each of your turns (max 8) and refills; it pays for cards.
    • Champions stay on the table. A fresh champion is sleepy (☾) and cannot attack until your next turn — unless it is Swift. Attacking a champion invites a strike back if it survives; attacking the Heart never does.
    • Guard champions must be attacked before any other target. Thorns sting attackers for 1. Drain champions mend their keeper's Heart by the damage they deal.
    • Kindle 🔥: the second card you play in a turn from a House you already played that turn is Kindled — champions enter at +1/+1, Tricks do their bigger printed effect. Sequence your plays House by House.
    • Tricks resolve at once: burn, bounce, buff, root, mend, sap.
    • An empty deck bites: every draw from it costs growing fatigue damage.
    • Vesper plays by readable rules — she kills what she can kill cleanly, hides behind Guards, mends when low and goes for your Heart when she can finish. Read her, and counter.
